CBECC Space PolyLoop Checking Tool

Detects missing space PolyLoops and area mismatches (>5% deviation from child floors) in CBECC input files (.cibd, .cibd10, .cibd19, .cibd22, .cibd25i), automatically generates clean boundary loops by unioning child floors, and heals model geometry.

  • Automatic detection of spaces missing PolyLp or with area mismatch (>5%)
  • Shapely union of child floors (IntFlr, ExtFlr, UndgrFlr)
  • Interactive 2D CAD canvas with zoom, pan, and existing vs union overlay
  • In-place coordinate editor (add, remove, adjust)
  • Safe unique entity ID allocation (PolyLoop & CartesianPoint)
  • One-click batch repair & direct .cibd export
